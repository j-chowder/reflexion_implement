World’s End was founded in 1100 by Queen Dahlia Claymore with a battalion led by her brother Vandhilt.
The Shard was discovered in 992 by explorer Chris Rudolf.
The Shard was officially founded in 1105 by King Vandhilt Claymore after being exiled from World’s End by his sister, Dahlia.
Millhouse Grove is a haven founded by defectors of World’s End, called the Shield Clan, in 1120. 
The Shield Clan later split into two factions in 1139, the Buckler Clan and the Kite Clan; The Kite Clan relocated to Ogrias’ Rest.
Ogrias’ Rest was founded in 1070 by Thomas Ogrias.
Thomas Ogrias married Daisy Claymore in 1074, changing his surname to Claymore.
Thomas and Daisy Claymore have twins, Dahlia and Vandhilt, born in 1078.
Iron Town was founded in 1300 by King Revolver.
King Revolver was killed in 1340 through a coup d'état by the Shadow Clan, led by Arch Shadow.
The Shadow Clan ruled Iron Town for 30 years, before being overthrown and exiled to the Underside Peaks by the Light Clan.
Marmog was founded in 1232, by the Marmot Clan – it is ruled by a council called the Marmotics.
Weasel deserted the Shadow Clan in 1365, before the Light Clan's overthrow, and joined the Marmot Clan.
Weasel leads the Marmot Clan in a coup d'état to seize control of Marmog in 1372; he now rules Marmog.
