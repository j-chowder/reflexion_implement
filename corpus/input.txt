World's End rose in the year 1100, founded under the rule of Queen Dahlia Claymore. She established it alongside a battalion led by her own brother, Vandhilt, whose forces stood behind the settlement's earliest days.
Long before any settlement stood there, the land was first charted in 992 by an explorer named Chris Rudolf, whose discovery marked its earliest recorded appearance.
The Shard was officially founded in 1105 by King Vandhilt Claymore, who had been exiled from World's End by his own sister, Dahlia, and carved out a new rule in the wake of that exile.
Millhouse Grove came into being in 1120, built as a haven by a band of defectors who had broken away from World's End. Those defectors took on a new identity, becoming known as the Shield Clan.
In 1139, an internal division split the Shield Clan into two rival factions, the Buckler Clan and the Kite Clan. The Kite Clan chose to leave, relocating to Ogrias' Rest in the aftermath.
Ogrias' Rest was founded in 1070 by Thomas Ogrias, establishing the settlement's place in history from its earliest beginnings.
In 1074, Thomas Ogrias married Daisy Claymore. He took her family name as his own afterward, setting aside the Ogrias name entirely.
Thomas and Daisy Claymore welcomed twins into their family in 1078: a daughter, Dahlia, and a son, Vandhilt.
Iron Town was founded in 1300, its beginnings tied to the rule of King Revolver.
King Revolver's reign came to a violent end in 1340, when the Shadow Clan, under the command of Arch Shadow, seized power through a coup d'état.
The Shadow Clan held power over Iron Town for 30 years, until the Light Clan rose against them, overthrowing their rule and driving them into exile at the Underside Peaks.
Marmog was founded in 1232 by the Marmot Clan, and its governance was placed in the hands of a ruling council known as the Marmotics.
Before the Light Clan's overthrow of the Shadow Clan, a member named Weasel deserted their ranks in 1365, choosing instead to join the Marmot Clan.
In 1372, Weasel led the Marmot Clan in a coup d'état, seizing control of Marmog for himself and taking up rule over the settlement.
